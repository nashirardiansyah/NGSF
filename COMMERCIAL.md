# Commercial licensing

NGSF is free for noncommercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Commercial use needs a separate paid license.

## Do I need a commercial license?

| Use | License needed |
|---|---|
| Learning, personal projects, hobby use | Free |
| A university, school, or public research institute running NGSF apps | Free |
| A charity, NGO, or government body running NGSF apps | Free |
| A student or lecturer building an app for a thesis or course | Free |
| A company using NGSF for its own operations (inventory, HR, sales, etc.) | Commercial |
| A freelancer or agency building NGSF apps for paying clients | Commercial |
| Selling NGSF, a modified NGSF, or a product built on it | Commercial |
| Offering NGSF as a hosted or paid service | Commercial |

If you're unsure, ask. Small and early-stage businesses are welcome to get in touch.
